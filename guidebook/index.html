A Wedding That Gives Back

Your Celebration Helps Restore North Star House

North Star House is cared for by the North Star Historic Conservancy, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Your rental directly supports the ongoing restoration and preservation of this Julia Morgan landmark, so it can keep welcoming the community for tours, events, and history for generations to come.

Every couple who celebrates here becomes part of the house’s story, and each wedding includes a personalized legacy brick placed on the grounds, a lasting mark of your day at North Star House.
